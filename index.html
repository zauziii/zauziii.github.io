About me

I’m a biostatistician at the University of Helsinki, where I advise researchers on study design, analysis, interpretation, and reporting, and support training in R and SPSS.

Previously, at the Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare (THL), I worked with population surveys and register data on disease risk factors, work ability, and future health needs.

Research & methods

Questions I work on and the methods I use.

01

Health over time

Understanding how outcomes change, when events occur, and how repeated observations fit together.

  • Longitudinal & mixed-effects models
  • Survival & time-to-event analysis
  • Clinical and register-based studies
02

Population health

Studying risk factors, inequalities, and the health needs of a changing population.

  • Survey and linked register data
  • Simulation & health projections
  • Multiple imputation
03

Research in practice

Helping research teams turn their questions into appropriate analyses and clear scientific reports.

  • Study design & statistical consultation
  • Reproducible analysis & visualization
  • Statistical teaching & training
